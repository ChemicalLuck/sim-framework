Support several NPCs per encounter: `npcIds` on the encounter effect, per-NPC needs and picks (optional `npcTurnOrder`), slotted `npcNeed.<slot>.*` / `npc.<slot>.*` conditions, action `target` slots, per-NPC view rows, and NPCs leaving while the rest carry on.
